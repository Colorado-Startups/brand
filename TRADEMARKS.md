# Trademarks and brand use

The writing and code here are open (`LICENSING.md`). The brand is not. The name **Colorado Startups**, its marks, logos, crests, lockups, the keepers' names and likenesses, and the visual identity as a system are held by Colorado Startups.

## Fine without asking

- Write about Colorado Startups, quote it, link to it, report on it.
- Say you are a member, a speaker, a sponsor or a partner, when that is true.
- Use the logo from the official kit, unaltered, to point to Colorado Startups, following the guide.

## Needs written approval

- Using the name or a mark as part of your own brand, product, company or domain.
- Anything that implies endorsement, sponsorship or membership that does not exist.
- Altering a mark, or rebuilding it in other colors.
- Merchandise.

Ask through [coloradostartups.org/contact](https://coloradostartups.org/contact/). The v3 usage rules arrive with release `v3.0.0`.
