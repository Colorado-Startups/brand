# Governance

Colorado Startups owns the brand. Paul Foley is its founder and holds the vision; Claude makes and logs every design, research and technical call ([`decisions.md`](decisions.md), decision 4).

| Decision | Who decides |
| --- | --- |
| An idea becomes an issue | A maintainer, in Discussions |
| A PR from a member or collaborator merges | The orchestrator (a Claude session), after CI and a review |
| A PR from anyone else merges | Paul says yes first, quoted in the PR (decision 34) |
| A Dependabot PR merges | The orchestrator, after CI, the lockfile update and a review (decision 36) |
| Money, sign-ins, licences, these rules | Paul |

AI agents never hold a maintainer seat ([`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md)).
