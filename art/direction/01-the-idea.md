# Art direction v5: Colorado, transformed with magic: The idea

Part 1 of 11 of [`DIRECTION.md`](../DIRECTION.md).

## The idea

Each place in the brand's world is a real Colorado landmark, repainted as a bright, hand-painted video-game world (decision 43). Magic transforms it: the landmark becomes a fantasy city that blends two named worlds, and the place's own people fill it (decision 44). Paul's goal: people in Colorado Startups "have fun and feel like they're in a video game" (decision 43).

| Order | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The place: epic, still recognisable, one hero structure at huge scale | Decisions 43 and 44 |
| 2 | Its community: its people, everywhere in the frame | Decision 44 |
| 3 | Two creatures, one wild and one mount, as small accents | Decision 44 |

All artwork is FIBO 2.0 and none is SVG. Video is LTX-2.5 and image motion is Rive (decision 37). Every card shows only its name, composited after the render in Aileron. There is no tagline and no text chip on the image (decision 46).

## What changed from v4

| v4 (2026-11-02) | v5 (this file) |

| The Hall, carved into the Front Range: granite halls, hearths, molten gold | Each real place transformed with magic and painted as a game world (decisions 43 and 44) |
