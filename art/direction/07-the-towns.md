## The towns

These are the real places behind the art. Five are rendered (decision 45); the others keep their facts here for later. The towns look like Colorado before the magic: brick and timber main streets, steep-roofed cottages for snow, mine headframes, plains windpumps, footbridges over creeks and aspens in the square.

| Town | Outpost | What a Coloradan recognizes | Place art (Part 2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boulder | Photon Mountain | The Flatirons: tilted slabs of red Fountain Formation sandstone above the town; Boulder Creek and its footbridges | An elven city and a World Tree on the Flatirons |
| Golden | Photon Mountain | Clear Creek running through town at the base of the Front Range, between the two Table Mountains; the Colorado School of Mines; gold-rush storefronts and headframes on the hills | A dwarven kingdom's gate in Table Mountain |
| Denver | Photon Mountain | City lights and the skyline on the plain, seen from the foothills; brick warehouses | A human citadel before the skyline |
| Colorado Springs | Space Peak | Pikes Peak over the city, in coarse pink to brick-red granite; the cog railway | A dwarven aerie on Pikes Peak's crags |
| Manitou Springs | Space Peak | A Victorian main street at the foot of Pikes Peak; its public mineral springs, as they are | Not rendered |
| Fort Collins | Fusion Canyon | Old Town by the Cache la Poudre River; Poudre Canyon; the tooth-shaped granite of Horsetooth Mountain on the western skyline | A gnome and hobbit town on the Poudre Canyon floor |
| Greeley | Fusion Canyon | A farm town on the High Plains where the Cache la Poudre meets the South Platte; wide sky, windpumps, the Front Range low in the west | Not rendered |

Arvada, Loveland and Windsor follow the same facts. `data/world.yaml` (S2) lists every town. Facts in this table come from the Sources in Part 11.
