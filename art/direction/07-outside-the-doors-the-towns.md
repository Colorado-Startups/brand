## Outside the doors: the towns

The great doors open onto real towns (`lore.md`). The forge is inside; outside is the town square, where founders meet their neighbors. The towns look like Colorado: brick and timber main streets, stone-and-timber cottages with steep roofs for snow, mine headframes, plains windpumps, footbridges over the creeks and aspens in the square. Dwarves and gnomes visit from the Hall. The townsfolk are founders and their neighbors.

| Town | Outpost | What a Coloradan recognizes |
|---|---|---|
| Boulder | Photon Mountain | The Flatirons: tilted slabs of red Fountain Formation sandstone above the town; Boulder Creek and its footbridges |
| Golden | Photon Mountain | Clear Creek running through town at the base of the Front Range, between the two Table Mountains; the Colorado School of Mines; gold-rush storefronts and headframes on the hills |
| Denver | Photon Mountain | City lights and the skyline on the plain, seen from the foothills; brick warehouses |
| Colorado Springs | Space Peak | Pikes Peak over the city, in coarse pink to brick-red granite; the cog railway |
| Manitou Springs | Space Peak | A Victorian main street at the foot of Pikes Peak; its public mineral springs, as they are |
| Fort Collins | Fusion Canyon | Old Town by the Cache la Poudre River; the tooth-shaped granite of Horsetooth Mountain on the western skyline |
| Greeley | Fusion Canyon | A farm town on the High Plains where the Cache la Poudre meets the South Platte; wide sky, windpumps, the Front Range low in the west |
