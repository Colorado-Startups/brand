## The five places

Each place keeps its real landmark and blends two named worlds into one city (decision 44). Files in `art/` may name the influences. Nothing that ships does (decision 44); `tools/franchise_gate.py` checks `data/`, `dist/`, `guide/`, `templates/` and `logo/`.

| Place (region) | Landmark kept | World of Warcraft + The Lord of the Rings | People, one blended people | Wild creature | Mount |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Boulder (Photon Mountain) | Flatirons | Mount Hyjal and its World Tree + Rivendell | Night elves + Noldor | Green dragons | Hippogryphs, golden-eagle style |
| Golden (Photon Mountain) | North and South Table Mountain | Ironforge + Erebor | Ironforge dwarves and gnomes + Durin's folk | Stone wyrms of boulders veined with gold | Dwarven steam tank drills |
| Denver (Photon Mountain) | Downtown skyline, Front Range | Stromgarde + Edoras | Humans + Rohirrim, with dwarves | White gryphons with bald-eagle heads | White horses glowing with the Light |
| Colorado Springs (Space Peak) | Pikes Peak | Aerie Peak + the Eagles' Eyrie | Wildhammer dwarves | Gryphons, golden-eagle style | Dwarven gyrocopters |
| Fort Collins (Fusion Canyon) | Poudre Canyon | New Tinkertown + the Shire | Gnomes + hobbits | Mechanical dragonlings | Mechanostriders |

Source: decisions 39 and 44. The prompts are in `PAINTED` in `art/render/fibo_cards.py`.

## Borrow and blend

| Idea | Source |
|---|---|
| A viewer sees both at once: the real landmark and the city it is paired with | Decision 39 |
| Borrow and blend; do not copy outright. "Similar enough that people get it" | Paul, decision 39 |
| The place first, epic; its community second, everywhere; the two creatures as small accents | Decision 44 |
| No place is a dark one. Photon Mountain is about light, so the Dark Iron dwarves and their forge city, and Moria, are left out | Decisions 39 and 40 |
| Kept for later: each place's hero creature, and Photon Mountain's region art (a white-and-gold light dragon, angels, paladins) | Decisions 44 and 48 |

## What binds

| Law or term | What it means for a render |
|---|---|
| Trademark | No franchise names or logos in shipped text or images |
| Copyright | Nothing copies a franchise's actual art |
| Bria's terms | We hold the rights to every image we upload, so no franchise art as input. One attribution call per image. No training on outputs. An AI-made label |
| Photo credits | CC BY credits ride in each image's metadata (decision 46) |
| LTX-2.5 licence | The duties for video are in `art/LICENSES.md` |

Source: decision 44, "What binds now".
