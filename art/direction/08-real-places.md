## Real places

The real places are transformed with magic (decisions 38, 43 and 44). Each place's landmark stays recognisable.

## Image inputs

FIBO 2.0 and LTX-2.5 may take any image we have the rights to: public-domain and CC0 photos (credit them), real photos of the Colorado landmarks, and our own art. Franchise art is not an input: Bria's terms require us to hold the rights to every image we upload (decision 44).
